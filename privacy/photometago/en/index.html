Privacy Policy - PhotoMetaGo

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Developer: Paolo Savio Videomaker e Fotografo

Privacy contact: info@paolosavio.eu

1. App purpose

PhotoMetaGo is a photography workflow app that can receive images from compatible cameras via FTP, apply IPTC/XMP metadata, manage metadata presets, and deliver photographs to destinations selected by the user, including Google Drive and FTP/FTPS/SFTP servers.

2. Data processed on the device

At the user's request, the app may access JPEG photographs, JSON/XMP preset files, photographic metadata and transfer settings. This information is normally processed locally on the device to provide the requested functions.

The current version of PhotoMetaGo does not use developer-operated advertising SDKs, analytics systems or crash-reporting services.

3. Photo transfers

When the user uses DELIVER, PhotoMetaGo transfers selected photographs to a destination configured by the user. Transfers occur only following a user action.

  • Google Drive: transfers use the official Google Drive APIs and the user's OAuth authorization.
  • FTPS/SFTP: data is transferred using encrypted protocols.
  • FTP: traditional FTP does not encrypt content or credentials in transit. It should only be used on networks and servers the user considers trusted. FTPS, SFTP or Google Drive are recommended for Internet transfers.

The PhotoMetaGo developer does not receive copies of transferred photographs and does not operate a PhotoMetaGo image-storage server.

4. Google Drive and Google user data

Google Drive connection is optional. PhotoMetaGo requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope solely to create, upload and manage files used by the app in the Google Drive account authorized by the user.

Google user data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide the visible Google Drive delivery feature. It is not sold or used for advertising, profiling or marketing analytics.

Use of information received from Google APIs is subject to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Credentials and settings

FTP/FTPS/SFTP settings, Google Drive OAuth tokens, presets and app preferences are stored locally in the app's private storage on the device. The developer does not receive this information.

6. Retention and deletion

Local photographs remain on the device or in the folder selected by the user until the user deletes them. Files uploaded to Google Drive or FTP/FTPS/SFTP servers remain at the selected destination and are governed by that destination's retention and privacy practices.

PhotoMetaGo does not create a developer-hosted PhotoMetaGo account. Users can remove local app data by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app. Disconnecting Google Drive removes locally stored authorization tokens. Uploaded files must be deleted from the selected destination by the user.

7. Sharing and sale

PhotoMetaGo does not sell personal data. Transfers to Google Drive or user-configured servers occur only at the user's request to provide the selected delivery function.

8. Security

PhotoMetaGo limits access to files selected by the user and uses encrypted protocols where available. The security of an FTP/FTPS/SFTP destination also depends on the configuration of the server and network used.

9. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated if app functions, third-party services, legal requirements or Google Play requirements change. The update date will be shown at the top of this page.

10. Contact

For privacy or app-related requests: info@paolosavio.eu.
